Methodology and Analytical Warnings

Reconciliation

CAPEX, Year 1 revenue, labour, OPEX, EBITDA, IRR, NPV and decision scores were mapped to workbook outputs.

Debt modelling

DSCR uses an annuity-style annual debt payment while the supporting debt balance declines linearly. Original workbook results are preserved.

Payback limitation

Payback is presented as the number of negative cumulative years rather than an exact fractional payback date.
